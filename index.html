Mesonet Explorer

Weather & soil data from the Montana Mesonet

What this shows

An interactive map of live and historical weather & soil observations from every Montana Mesonet station. Each dot is a station, colored by the variable you pick; the legend gives the scale.

What the terms mean
  • HydroMet / AgriMet — the Mesonet's two sub-networks: core weather & water-supply stations, and agriculture-focused stations with expanded soil sensing.
  • Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) — the drying power of the air; higher means faster water loss from plants and soil.
  • Comprehensive Climate Index (CCI) — a livestock weather-stress index combining temperature, humidity, wind, and solar radiation.
  • Soil VWC — volumetric water content: the share of the soil's volume that is water.
  • Percent Saturation — how full the soil's pore space is with water, relative to its maximum.
  • Soil Water Potential — how tightly the soil holds its remaining water; higher values mean drier soil that roots must work harder to draw from.
  • Bulk EC — the soil's electrical conductivity, which tracks salinity and moisture.
  • Δ VWC — the change in soil water content over a trailing window; positive is wetting, negative is drying.
  • Reference ET — the water a well-watered grass surface would lose to evaporation and transpiration; a baseline for irrigation planning.
  • Growing Degree Days (GDD) — accumulated warmth above a crop base temperature, used to track crop development.
  • Stale — a station whose latest report is 2 hours old or more (usually a connectivity gap); hidden by default in Latest mode.

How to use it

  • Variable — the dropdown picks what the dots show: air, wind, precipitation, soil, ET, and groundwater measurements.
  • Time — Latest is live and auto-refreshes; Hourly and Daily let you pick any date.
  • A station — tap or click a dot for its value, details, a photo, and a link to its dashboard.
Time modes in detail
  • Latest — most recent observation at each station, auto-refreshed. Stations whose last report is 2 hours old or more are hidden as stale (toggle them back in the legend).
  • Hourly — pick any date and hour to see hourly values across the network.
  • Daily — pick any date to see daily aggregates (totals for precipitation, means for most other variables).
All controls & shortcuts
  • Variable — the list adapts to the time mode: precipitation accumulations and soil-moisture change appear in Latest, reference ET in Hourly/Daily, and growing degree days in Daily.
  • Search — press / to jump there; ↑/↓ + Enter to pick a station. (Add ?kbd=off to the URL to disable the / shortcut.)
  • Networks — chips toggle HydroMet / AgriMet stations.
  • Hover a dot for the station's value; click for details, a photo, and a dashboard link. On a phone the details fill the screen under the bar; close them with × (or Back) to pick another station on the map.
  • Stacked stations — a small count badge marks sites with more than one station; hovering or clicking fans them out, and the details panel lists the others under "Also at this site".
  • Units — °F/°C toggles between U.S. and metric units.
  • Legend — click the No-data / Stale rows to show or hide those stations; the Overlays chips add counties, watersheds, or live radar.
  • Color scale — click the color strip to pick a different ramp or set a custom range; the pin locks the scale in place while you step through dates and hours. "≤"/"≥" on the ends means a few extreme values share the end colors.
  • Export — download a branded PNG of the current view (fixed Montana framing, color scale included).
  • On a phone — the bar keeps the variable picker and search; the ☰ menu at its right edge holds the time controls, networks, legend, units, labels, export, share, theme, and this guide.
  • Esc closes the station details, the stacked-station fan-out, and the menu, one at a time.

Share or bookmark

The address bar reflects every choice, so a bookmark reopens exactly this view. The share button copies a link that pins everything, theme and camera included, so the person you send it to sees what you see. ?station=<id> opens a single station.

Data

Data come live from the Montana Mesonet API v2. Radar imagery courtesy of the Iowa Environmental Mesonet. Colour maps include Fabio Crameri's Scientific colour maps. The Montana Mesonet is operated by the Montana Climate Office at the University of Montana.
